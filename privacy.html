Privacy Policy

Newport CRM (Newport Specialty Partners acquisition CRM). Last updated October 29, 2026.

Newport CRM is a web app for tracking acquisition opportunities, tasks, contacts, documents, meetings, and related email. It is built by a UT Dallas student team for Newport Specialty Partners. This page explains what data the app uses and how it handles that data.

Sign-in

You sign in with a Microsoft account. The app receives your name and email address from Microsoft to identify you. The app never sees your password.

Gmail and Outlook data

If you choose to connect Gmail or Outlook, the app asks for permission to read, organize, and send email in that mailbox. It uses this access only to:

  • show your mail inside the CRM's Email page,
  • mark messages read or unread, move them between folders, and save drafts when you ask it to,
  • send messages that you write and send from the CRM, and
  • show new-mail alerts in the CRM.

Mail is fetched directly from Google or Microsoft by your own browser. Mail content and the access token stay in your browser on your device. They are not sent to the Newport CRM server. The Google access token lasts one hour and is kept only for the current browser tab. Signing out of the CRM erases the cached mail and tokens from that browser.

Calendar and OneDrive

If you use Meetings or Documents, the app reads and writes your Microsoft calendar events and stores CRM files in a "Newport CRM" folder in your OneDrive. This happens directly between your browser and Microsoft.

CRM records

Opportunities, tasks, contacts, document records, and activity notes that you enter are saved to the Newport CRM server so approved team members can see the same records. Mail content is not saved there.

Google user data

Newport CRM's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app does not sell Google user data, does not use it for advertising, does not use it to train AI models, and does not transfer it to anyone except as needed to show it to you inside the app.

Removing access

You can disconnect Gmail from the Email page at any time. You can also remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and to your Microsoft account at account.live.com/consent/Manage (personal accounts) or through your organization's settings. To have your CRM records deleted from the server, email the contact below.
